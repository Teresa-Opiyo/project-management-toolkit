Project Management Toolkit

Free browser tools by Teresa Akumu Opiyo. Your data stays in your browser and is never uploaded.

Risk Register

Add risks, rate probability and impact from 1 to 5, and see them ranked and placed on a heat map.

Task Tracker

Track tasks, owners and deadlines. Overdue tasks are highlighted and progress updates automatically.

Status Report Generator

Fill in the form. The report pulls your top risks and task progress automatically. Copy it into an email or print it.

  







How to use


  1. Risk Register: add each risk with probability and impact. Score = probability x impact. 15 or more is High, 8 to 14 is Medium, below 8 is Low.
    2. 

  2. Task Tracker: add tasks with an owner and due date, then change status as work progresses.
    3. 

  3. Status Report: fill the form and copy the formatted report. It adds your top three risks and the task summary for you.
    4. 

  4. Your data: saved in this browser only (local storage). Use Download CSV to keep a copy. Clearing browser data removes it.


Guides and worked examples are in the repository folders docs and examples. The sample data in this tool is fictional.
